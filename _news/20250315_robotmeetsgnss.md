Together with our colleagues from the **Hong Kong Polytechnic University**, we are organizing a new workshop series, launching at **IEEE IROS** this year, titled "<a href='https://robotmeetsgnss.com'>Robot Meets GNSS</a>."

Our goal is to foster collaboration between the Robotics and Navigation communities, highlighting the synergy between these fields.
